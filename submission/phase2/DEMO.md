# Live demo: try Qart.ai with two synthetic CVs

Live app: **https://qartai.replit.app** (no account needed). Recorded demo: https://youtu.be/_TpEkTakf44

Both CVs are synthetic and labelled; no real person's data is used.

| CV | Who | Download |
|---|---|---|
| Daniel | AP Analyst, 5 years, working | [daniel.docx](../../data/samples/cv_files/daniel.docx) |
| Aina | Final-year finance student | [aina.docx](../../data/samples/cv_files/aina.docx) |

## Daniel (working adult whose job AI is changing)

1. Open the app, choose **Upload CV** and upload `daniel.docx`. Qart reads his role and 10 skills,
   each with the CV sentence it came from.
2. **My options**: future roles ranked by fit and the job-market forecast (Procurement Analyst,
   P2P Reporting Analyst, ...). Open **Why?** on a role to see the score, the skills gap and the
   sources.
3. **My plan** (no role chosen): *Your job's AI-era path*. Each step is one of his skills, marked
   "check and steer AI-run work" or "do it faster with AI", with checked courses and YouTube
   videos on doing that work with AI (for example Invoice Processing → AI Builder invoice
   extraction). Each item shows the quote that proves AI is used on the work.
4. Choose **Procurement Analyst** as the destination: the plan becomes *Your path to Procurement
   Analyst*.
5. **Live intelligence → Change the trend data → Job-market trend**: set Clerical to −1.0 and
   apply. Roles change category (for example Transforming → Shrinking) and the ranking updates;
   nothing is stored.

## Aina (student)

1. Upload `aina.docx`. Qart reads her as a student with 9 skills.
2. **My options**: Budget Analyst is ranked first (forecast: Growing).
3. **My plan**: *Your skills' AI-era path*, 7 steps built from the skills she already has.

Every number on screen is labelled **observed** (with source and date), **assumption** or
**estimate**; the person makes every decision.
